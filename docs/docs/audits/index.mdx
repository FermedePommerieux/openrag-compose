# Audits and benchmarks

These artifacts are evidence for behavior at their recorded revisions and
environments. They are not normative product documentation. Current contracts
are defined by the architecture pages and current code; historical reports are
not silently rewritten when implementation status changes.

## Reading historical evidence safely

An older report may say a component is “not connected” because that was true
for its phase. Do not promote that sentence into current product status. Use
the current [architecture overview](/architecture/overview),
[structured metadata search](/product/structured-metadata-search), and
[versioned contract index](/reference/versioned-contracts) for normative
behavior.
