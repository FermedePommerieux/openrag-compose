# Truth and provenance contract

This page is normative for user-facing claims in the Pommerieux fork. The
states below are deliberately not interchangeable.

| State | Meaning | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ASSERTED` | A source or trusted ingestion boundary explicitly supplied an identity or relation accepted by the contract | The asserted content is universally true |
| `OBSERVED` | A named extractor or system reported a source-qualified value | The value is preferred over conflicting observations |
| `ASSOCIATED` | Two occurrences share bounded, recorded association evidence | One derives from, versions or duplicates the other |
| `INFERRED` | A reproducible hypothesis was derived from evidence | A strong provenance edge exists |
| `UNKNOWN` | Required usable evidence is absent or indeterminate | The predicate is false |
| `CONFLICTING` | Preserved observations disagree under the policy | One value has been silently selected |
| `INVALID` | An observation cannot satisfy its syntax or semantic contract | The underlying real-world fact is known to be absent |

These labels are implemented by the
[`InvestigationStatus`](https://github.com/FermedePommerieux/openrag-compose/blob/975f933fa3149dd60a0f481d5a51ae184d6ddf0d/src/models/document_investigation.py)
model and used by metadata and association projections.

## Mandatory interpretations

- **Metadata observation is not factual truth.** PDF Info, XMP, OOXML, EML,
  EXIF/IPTC/XMP, archive, filesystem and ingestion systems can be wrong,
  missing, copied or spoofed. The observation retains its source, trust class,
  normalization status and time of extraction.
- **Document association is not provenance derivation.** A shared creator,
  date, source, type, parent, filename or hash is recorded only with its exact
  association dimension and evidence status.
- **Same author/date/type is not same document.** Stable occurrence identity
  and binary identity remain separate from descriptive metadata.
- **Candidate lineage is not `prov:wasDerivedFrom`.** Candidate-lineage
  projection is internal evidence only. It emits no strong relation.
- **An association neighborhood is not certifiable closure.** It is bounded,
  explicitly `scope_expanding=false`, and is not an input to the v1 coverage
  decision.
- **`coverage.complete` is not physical-archive completeness.** It certifies
  the accessible documentary closure reached from discovered seeds under one
  versioned policy and index view.

## Strong provenance, weak association and candidate lineage

Strong provenance is a validated explicit relation in the fork's bounded
PROV-O profile. Even then, the `documentary-prov-o` policy decides whether the
exact role/source-type/target-type triple may expand scope. Unknown triples
fail closed.

Weak metadata association is descriptive proximity. It can help an analyst
navigate, but broad dimensions such as a source system, year or MIME type can
form mega-hubs. Association dimensions are therefore bounded and do not
automatically enlarge coverage scope.

Candidate lineage records why two occurrences might merit review. It is not
activated as trusted lineage, does not create `derived_from`, and does not
change PROV-O closure.

## Conceptual example

```text
email message
    ↑ attachment_of (explicit, typed, policy-approved)
binary attachment
    └── other explicit provenance relations

same month / same observed creator
    └── association evidence only
        └── no strong provenance edge
```

For OpenArchiver attachments, the attachment has its own stable binary identity
such as `urn:openrag:openarchiver:attachment:<attachment_id>`. A parent-mail URL
is a navigation location; it is not the binary attachment identity.

## Contradiction and absence

The answer layer distinguishes supported evidence, not found under certified
scoped coverage, not retrieved, incomplete coverage and contradiction. A
capable model cannot convert missing evidence or an incomplete certificate into
a documentary absence claim. When sources conflict, the system preserves and
attributes the conflict instead of choosing a silent truth.
